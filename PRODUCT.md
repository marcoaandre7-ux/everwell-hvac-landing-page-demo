# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Confirmed: static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with no framework or build step required.

## Users

The primary users are owners and managers of local HVAC companies in the United States, especially regional businesses with roughly 5–30 technicians. They need a credible, reusable marketing page that turns urgent service demand, missed calls, and ad traffic into booked appointments.

The page visitor is a homeowner or local business operator who needs HVAC repair, installation, maintenance, or indoor-air-quality service and wants a fast, trustworthy path to request help.

## Product Purpose

Create a reusable HVAC landing-page template that can be adapted for multiple local service companies. Success means a visitor quickly understands the service area and offer, feels confident enough to make contact, and can call or request an appointment with minimal friction.

## Positioning

The template presents an HVAC company as a responsive local service operation and makes booking the central interaction. It is designed to connect later to CRM, scheduling, missed-call recovery, SMS, email, and lead-source tracking instead of functioning as a purely decorative brochure.

## Operating Context

The page will commonly receive traffic from Google search, paid campaigns, local listings, and mobile users with an urgent heating or cooling problem. Calls and appointment requests are the main conversion events. The template must remain understandable and usable on mobile devices under time pressure.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Reusable static template with easily replaceable business name, phone, service area, services, hours, and calls to action.
- Primary action: request service or book an appointment; direct phone calling is a parallel high-priority action.
- Initial version may demonstrate form behavior locally but does not claim a live CRM, calendar, or messaging integration.
- Brand and business details are fictional placeholders and must be clearly replaceable.
- No fabricated testimonials, customer counts, certifications, response-time guarantees, or performance statistics.

## Brand Commitments

Use a fictional HVAC brand for the demonstration. The voice should be direct, reassuring, local, and commercially credible in American English. No permanent real-company brand assets are established.

## Evidence on Hand

The supplied strategy summary supports the HVAC niche, the lead-conversion positioning, the intended customer profile, and planned capabilities such as CRM, scheduling, missed-call automation, reminders, review requests, and reporting. No real testimonials, case studies, customer logos, certifications, or business performance data were supplied and none may be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Make the next action obvious within seconds.
- Treat urgent mobile calling and scheduled service requests as equally legitimate paths.
- Build trust through clarity, process, and practical service information rather than invented social proof.
- Keep every company-specific detail easy to replace.
- Prepare the front end for future CRM and automation integrations without pretending those integrations already exist.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, keyboard-visible focus states, reduced-motion support, readable contrast, and responsive layouts that remain usable on small mobile screens.
